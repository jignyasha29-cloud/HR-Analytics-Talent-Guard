# HR Analytics | Talent Guard – Attrition Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

**Talent Guard** is an HR Analytics dashboard developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee attrition patterns and identify key factors influencing employee turnover.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of attrition across departments, job roles, tenure bands, income levels, overtime status and employee demographics.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition and attrition rate
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition
- Understand the relationship between employee income and attrition
- Analyze attrition based on employee tenure
- Compare employees who left with those who stayed
- Identify key HR insights that can support employee retention strategies

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- Data Visualization
- HR Analytics
- Data Analysis
- KPI & Dashboard Design

## 📌 Key KPIs

- **Total Employees:** 1,000
- **Total Attrition:** 237
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
- **Average Monthly Income:** $7K
- **Average Years – Employees Who Left:** 5.1
- **Average Years – Employees Who Stayed:** 7.4

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Sales** has the highest department-wise attrition rate at approximately **20.6%**.
- Employees with **0–2 years of tenure** show the highest attrition rate at **29.82%**.
- Employees working **overtime** have significantly higher attrition than non-overtime employees.
- Employees who left have a lower average monthly income compared with employees who stayed.
- **Sales Representatives** show one of the highest job-role attrition rates.
- Employees who left have a lower average tenure compared with employees who stayed.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Department-wise Attrition Rate
- Total Employees by Attrition
- Average Monthly Income by Attrition
- Income by Job Level & Attrition
- Tenure Band Attrition Analysis
- Employee Count by Job Role
- Attrition by Job Role
- Interactive filters for:
  - Gender
  - Job Role
  - Overtime
  - Department
